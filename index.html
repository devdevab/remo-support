Remo is a simple iPhone camera with one signature look and photographs framed to fit your screen.

Need a hand?

Report a problem or ask a question using our GitHub support page. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Reports are public: please do not attach private photographs, passwords, location details, or personal information.

Contact support

Taking photographs

Allow Camera access in iOS Settings. Tap the live image or shutter to capture. Hold an area of the image to guide focus and exposure. Use the optional grid and zoom controls to frame your photograph.

Saving and your library

Allow Add Photos access for automatic saving to Apple Photos. Remo also stores captures in its local library. Processing may take longer for RAW or high-resolution captures. You can keep shooting while supported captures develop. Open the library to review, share, or delete photographs. Hold a photograph to compare, swipe to browse, and close the preview to return to the camera.

Quality and speed

Choose quality-first capture or faster bursts in Settings. Available capture formats and resolution depend on your iPhone, zoom, and lighting. Cropping a photograph to the screen’s shape reduces the final pixel count.

Keeping your photographs

Deleting a photograph in Remo removes its local copy; a copy already saved to Apple Photos remains there. Removing the app removes its local library. Keep important photographs in Apple Photos or export them before removing the app.
